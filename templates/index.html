About

Kowalski trading assistant is built with the trader in mind. Kowalski's main goal is to help a trader attain trading mastery, achieved through tools like a trade journal and a modern trade calculator.

Kowalski is designed and built by a trader, tailor-made for a trader's needs. The trading journal is designed to uncover valuable insights from your trading via in-depth journaling of trades. The modern trading calculator is designed to help a trader mathematically derive the required metrics for trading.
